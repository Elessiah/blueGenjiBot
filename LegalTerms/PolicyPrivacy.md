# Privacy Policy — BlueGenji Bot

**Last updated**: 13 November 2026

This policy informs you of the data processed by the Discord bot **BlueGenji Bot**, why it is processed, how long it is kept, who receives it and what your rights are (Articles 13 and 14 GDPR).

## 02. Data processed and purposes

### Relayed advertisements

ID of the original message and of its author, date, IDs of the relayed copies and of their channels: they are used to relay the advertisement, to pass on its edits or deletion, to apply the cooldown between two advertisements, for the message count of **/stats**, for the bot's dashboard statistics and, on an exclusion, to find and remove the copies of the excluded user's advertisements. **Message content is not stored in the Bot's database**: it is copied, with its author's name, into the channels of partner servers, where their members read it. These copies are Discord messages: deleting the original advertisement within 7 days also deletes its copies; after that, they remain until the administrators of the server holding them delete them.

### Scrims and recruitment

For the **/scrim** and **/recrute** commands: author ID, game, level or role sought, server and date, which feed the activity statistics (**/stats** command, which only shows each user their own activity, and the bot's dashboard). After 30 days these rows are folded into plain counts per day, server and level (or role), without the author's ID; these counts are kept with no time limit, as a history of the Bot's activity. The level (Débutant, Intermédiaire, Avancé) and the role (Tank, DPS, Heal, Coach, Manager) are chosen from a closed list; a value typed as free text before this rule has been mapped onto the list choice it named exactly, and otherwise replaced with "unspecified", including in the per-day counts.

### Relay exclusions

IDs of the excluded user and of the moderator, date, and a reference to the log message holding the reason. The IDs of the excluded user and of the moderator, and the reason, are posted in the staff's private log channel; the reason is also sent by direct message to the Bot's owner. These messages are deleted, in the channel and in the direct messages alike, when the exclusion is lifted (section 03, which details the case of an exclusion issued before this rule). An exclusion applies to the **whole network** of partner servers: this is community moderation, decided (**/ban-user-of-this-server**, **/ban-user-of-another-server**) and lifted (**/unban**) by the administrators — and the holders of the Bot administration role — of any server with at least 50 members (bot accounts included) where the Bot is installed, as well as, from those same servers, by the Bot's owner and the association's presidency; the association's servers are exempt from this threshold (« Relay moderation » section of the Terms of Service). The **/ban-list** command therefore shows the full list of network exclusions (usernames, reason, date, ID) to the administrators of any server where the Bot is installed — including a server one creates oneself to invite it — and to the holders of the Bot administration role, so that they know who can no longer post through the Bot and why.

### Memberships and scheduled reminders

- **Association memberships** (commands restricted to the association's servers): when a member's membership is validated, the Bot sends them the confirmation by direct message, with the membership certificate if one is attached, without keeping it; it then records a reminder for the membership's expiry date — which means keeping, until that reminder, the fact that this member belongs to the association and until when.
- **Scheduled reminders** (same commands): ID of the targeted member or role and of the author, message, next sending date and frequency.

### Server configuration

IDs of the configured servers, channels and roles, the server's invite, and the ID of the administrator who set the invite or the referee role.

### Messages from the BlueGenji website

The website sends the Bot a Discord ID or username, the recipient's BlueGenji username, for the code only (login, certification or change of the Discord tag) the language of the BlueGenji account already linked to that Discord account (French or English, in which the Bot writes the code; without a linked account, the code is sent in French then in English), and the message to deliver (login code, match time or reminder, notice of removal from a match (“Match retiré”), check-in of a self-managed match sent to those who manage its entrants (or to the player of a solo entry), notice to a caster that someone else removed their registration for a match, referee alert, report; moderation notice — a report naming you, a team logo masked, removed, deleted or restored, an avatar hidden, removed or restored, the suspension of your account or its lifting —; join request for a team you manage; a team's invitation, the takeover of a ghost team the staff offers you or the ownership of your team transferred to you, which only name the team; data-protection notice); the Bot delivers personal messages (code, match time, reminder, removal from a match, match check-in, removal of a cast, moderation notice, join request, team invitation, takeover or ownership, data-protection notice) by direct message **without storing them**; those about matches (time, reminder, removal from a match, match check-in, removal of a cast) only go to accounts with a Discord tag certified on the website — an uncertified tag, even one linked by signing in with Discord, receives none, to avoid spam; each recipient (player, member of an entrant or caster) can turn off those about their matches (time, reminder, removal from a match, match check-in, removal of a cast) in “My profile” on the website. Referee alerts and reports, which name no player (team names and tournament links only), are also posted in the staff's private log channel; referee alerts are also sent by direct message to the members of the referee role of every server that has set one (**/set-referee-role**), reports to the association's management. When a streamed match starts and ends, the website also sends the Bot the text of the evening's announcement (names of both teams — in a solo tournament, each player's public BlueGenji username, “a player” for an entry without an account, a deleted account or a registration made before 16 October 2026 —, tournament name, time, state and score of each match, stream link), which the Bot posts in the #live channel of the association's Discord server, in one message per evening (four hours from the first live stream) that lists the matches live, those scheduled until the end of the evening whose broadcast is set to open at their start time and those finished (“Terminé”, with their score), and that it edits at each match start and end, up to four hours after the evening ends (a new message without a mention takes over when it can no longer be edited), with an @everyone mention for the association's official channel or a mention of the StreamViewer role only for any other channel. The website's direct messages are written in the language of the recipient's BlueGenji account, or as a single message in French then in English when it is not known (the association's leadership, members of the referee role, an unknown account), in French alone when both do not fit in one message; messages posted in channels stay in French. This processing falls under the [website's privacy policy](https://bluegenji-esport.fr/en/rgpd).

### Logs

The public activity feed on the bot's page contains no Discord ID; it repeats the time, the server name and the level or role chosen with **/scrim** or **/recrute**. The staff's private log channel and the server logs receive the names of servers that add or remove the Bot, operating errors, which may mention a Discord ID, and the website's activity journal (sign-ups, matches, tournaments, account deletion requests and their reactivation, “a player”), written by the website without any player's username. The Bot no longer writes usernames there on its own (messages older than this rule may quote some): the reason for an exclusion, free text written by the moderator, may quote one, and a failed direct-message delivery may mention the account concerned.

### Team voice channels for matches

In BlueGenji tournaments with that option, the Bot creates on the association's Discord server one voice channel per team of a match, under the name the website sends it (the team's name, see the website's messages above), returns to the website only that channel's ID, and deletes it once handed back and empty. It keeps a record of it: channel ID, key of the match and team (numbers, no name), creation and hand-back times. To know whether a channel is empty, it holds in memory at all times, whether or not a tournament has the option on, without ever recording it or sending it to the website, the voice state of the members of the association's server (Discord ID of the member and of their voice session, channel they are in, microphone or sound muted by themselves or by moderation, screen sharing or camera on, request to speak and stage suppression), with the member profile Discord attaches to it (including username, nickname, avatar and roles). It only uses it to know whether a channel is empty and, when the website's referees, or the team's owner, a manager or its captain, clear a team's channel, to disconnect all its occupants (the channel stays open): it keeps nothing of it, logs nothing of it and never tells the website how many people are in it, nor who. The channels are open to whoever sees the match category; Discord's audit log keeps the disconnections (their number and the clearing's reason, as Discord records them). The details are in the [“Team voice channels” paragraph of the website's privacy policy](https://bluegenji-esport.fr/en/rgpd#salons-vocaux) (processing activity T18).

### Legal basis

This processing is based on the association's **legitimate interest** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): running the relay between partner servers, moderating it and measuring its activity. Team voice channels also rest on its legitimate interest (smooth running of tournaments). Messages from the website rely on the legal basis of their original processing, set out in the website's register: legitimate interest for a player's match direct messages (time, reminders, “Match retiré”), the check-in of a self-managed match sent to those who manage its entrants (or to the player of a solo entry) and the notice sent to a caster whose registration someone else removes, which each of them objects to with the switch in “My profile” — the website keeps that choice under a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR), to keep honouring that objection; legitimate interest (organising competitions, the website's register T03) for team direct messages — join request, a team's invitation, offered takeover of a ghost team, transferred team ownership —, which anyone may object to on request (the website's “Report a problem” form, GDPR category); performance of the service the player registers for, for their public username in the #live announcement of a solo tournament; the association's legitimate interest in moderating its server for the AutoMod alert of such an announcement when blocked or flagged, deleted on request.

## 03. Retention periods

- **Tracking of relayed advertisements** (IDs, date): 7 days; it is erased at the first relay after that deadline, and at the latest during the night or when the Bot restarts. The copies posted in partner channels remain on Discord (section 02).
- **Scrims and recruitment**: 30 days with the author's ID; at the clean-up of the following night (or a restart of the Bot), the author's ID is erased and the rows are folded into counts per day, server and level (or role), kept with no time limit as a history of the Bot's activity.
- **Exclusions**: the exclusion record, until it is lifted; the notice and the reason posted in the staff's private log channel, and the reason copied by direct message to the Bot's owner, are deleted when it is lifted. For an exclusion issued before this rule, only the reason posted in the channel is: the rest follows the log channel's period (below).
- **Public activity feed** (time, server, level or role of each event): 30 days, deleted at the clean-up of the following night.
- **Server configuration** (relayed channels and their rank filters, the invite and the referee role with the ID of whoever set them, the Bot administration role, enabled modules): until the administrators remove it, and at the latest until the Bot leaves the server, which erases it. A departure while the Bot is down, which Discord does not notify, is caught up when it restarts.
- **Memberships and scheduled reminders**: until the reminder's last sending (for a membership, its expiry date) or its deletion, and at the latest until the Bot leaves the server where they were recorded, which erases them — including a departure while the Bot is down, caught up when it restarts.
- **Staff private log channel** (and the Bot's direct messages to its owner): one year (365 days); older messages from the Bot are deleted by the nightly clean-up, in batches (several nights for a large backlog), except the reason posted in the channel for an exclusion still in force — and, for an exclusion issued from this rule on, its notice and the copy of its reason by direct message —, deleted when it is lifted.
- **Team voice channels**: the channel is deleted as soon as it is empty once handed back, within 24 hours and 30 minutes of being handed back at the latest even if occupied, unless Discord prevents it; never handed back, it is deleted if it is empty 48 hours after its creation or its reuse; its record goes with it, or is forgotten 96 hours after its creation or its reuse if the Bot can no longer read the channel, and stays with no limit while a channel never handed back stays occupied or while Discord prevents its deletion (retried at each sweep; a channel the Bot no longer sees is left to moderation). These periods are accurate to within one sweep (every 30 minutes). A channel moved out of the match category is no longer tracked: its record is forgotten and the Bot no longer deletes it. Voice state and member profiles are only held in memory, kept up to date by Discord, and lost when the Bot stops. Discord's audit log keeps the creation and deletion of each channel, with its name, and the disconnections of a clearing (their number and reason, as Discord records them), for the period Discord sets.
- **Server logs**: according to their automatic rotation.
- **#live announcements** (evening message of the streamed matches: team names or, in a solo tournament, the players' public BlueGenji username): kept in the channel as long as it exists, even after the player's account is deleted; deleted at the request of the team or player concerned (the website's “Report a problem” form, GDPR category). An announcement blocked or flagged by an AutoMod rule: its alert stays in the moderation alert channel until the moderators delete it, and is deleted on request in the same way.
- **Backups**: the Bot's database is backed up twice a week (Sunday and Tuesday evenings), encrypted, and each copy is permanently deleted after 30 days at most. A restore is made from these archives, decrypted on the Bot's machine: the database no longer travels through Discord; the copy of the previous database it leaves alongside is deleted at the next successful restore, and at the latest during the night after it turns 30 days old.

## 06. Your rights

You have the following rights over your data:

- **Access**: find out what data the Bot keeps about you and obtain a copy.
- **Rectification**: have inaccurate data corrected.
- **Erasure**: have your data deleted; some Bot features may then no longer be available to you.
- **Restriction**: have the use of data frozen while a dispute is examined.
- **Objection**: object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interest. A player's username in the #live announcement of a solo tournament is not covered: it rests on performance of the service they register for — not being named in it means not registering; a past announcement is deleted on request (the website's “Report a problem” form, GDPR category). Nor is the site's keeping of your match direct messages choice: it rests on a legal obligation, that of continuing to honour your objection to those messages.

The right to data portability does not apply to the Bot's own processing: it is based on legitimate interest, not on consent or a contract; for the website's messages it delivers, see the [website's privacy policy](https://bluegenji-esport.fr/en/rgpd#exercer-vos-droits). To exercise your rights, use the means listed in the Contact section; you will receive a reply within one month.

If you consider that your rights are not respected, you may lodge a complaint with the [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes), the French data protection authority.

## 07. Security

- The Bot's database lives on the technical host's machine, access to which is restricted to the technical manager (SSH key authentication).
- Exchanges between the website and the Bot stay on that machine and are protected by a token.
- Backups are encrypted on that machine before any upload.

No measure makes a system infallible: in the event of a data breach likely to put you at risk, the association is required to notify the CNIL and, if the risk is high, the people concerned (Articles 33 and 34 GDPR).
